About Me

The Person Behind

I'm passionate about crafting meaningful designs that empower people and improve everyday experiences.

I'm Marco, a 19-year-old photographer and graphic designer who loves capturing moments and turning ideas into visual stories. Whether I'm behind the camera or working on a design, I focus on creativity, emotion, and detail to make every piece feel meaningful. I enjoy experimenting with different styles, learning new techniques, and pushing myself to grow with every project. For me, photography and graphic design aren't just skills — they're ways to express how I see the world and connect with people through art.
